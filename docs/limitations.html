Project limitations (beyond flags)

  • No salary archive yet. The expectation model needs historical (salary, points) pairs — start exporting contest CSVs daily from your own accounts.
  • No ownership feed. Contest-dashboard leverage requires projected/historical ownership; not publicly available.
  • Single-codec scope. Engine currently ships NFL scoring tables; other sports need their official scoring transcribed (follow the S33/S34 pattern).
  • Simulator calibration. Volatility/field-shape parameters are original defaults, not validated against real contest results yet (roadmap: backtest).
  • Static pricing. Any pricing shown on this site is a dated snapshot with its source — verify live before relying.
  • No affiliation. This is independent research; trademarks belong to their owners.

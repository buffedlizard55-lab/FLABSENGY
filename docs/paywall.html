Paywall & legal

The request said “rebuild anything that is behind a paywall.” Here is exactly where the line was drawn, and why — with the governing clauses linked.

Governing terms (verified) The Action Network’s Terms of Use explicitly cover www.fantasylabs.com (S27). They prohibit — in their words — “page-scrape” / “robot” / “spider” access, reproduction/mirroring of Content without written consent, and “reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble” of source code. §1 also claims ownership of “fantasy-related player salaries … historical player performance … fantasy trends, fantasy systems … picks, articles”.

Decision matrix

Asked forVerdictWhat was done instead
Bypass paywall / access subscription content DECLINED I1 No login obtained, no paywall circumvention attempted. Paywalled article bodies never accessed. S27
Scrape / crawl fantasylabs.com DECLINED I2 Research = a small number of direct public page reads (like manual browsing). No robots probing, no bulk crawling, no API abuse. S27
Reverse-engineer their code/clients DECLINED I2 Their source never touched. System behavior was studied only from public docs/articles. S27
Rebuild the projection system DONE — clean-room Original engine/ implements the publicly documented definitions (glossaries S11–S14) and described behavior (S16–S21) with our own code, our own weights, our own tests.
Copy their projections / ownership / Pro Trends DECLINED I3, I15 Not public; would be infringement. Listed as residual gaps in Roadmap.
Reach “same data quality” PARTIAL — lawful inputs Same metric rigor + open data (nflverse CC-BY, official APIs, your own DK/FD CSV exports). Not their proprietary feeds. Data sources

What “rebuild” means here

  • Ideas they published in the open (metric formulas, R² semantics, trend outputs, optimizer input list, sim pipeline steps) → reimplemented as original code with source citations in docstrings.
  • Short attributed quotes from public glossaries used to document definitions — each with its ledger ID.
  • Nothing behind the subscription wall was read, let alone republished.
  • No FantasyLabs data ships in this repo — not salaries, not projections, not ownership, not articles.

Third-party legal notes

  • DraftKings/FanDuel data: no official public DFS salaries API; use your own contest CSV exports; unofficial endpoint clients are unstable and ToS-risky (S40 S41 — flag I16).
  • League data: nflverse is CC-BY-4.0 (attribute!); MLB Stats API is free but redistribution terms deserve a read; NBA endpoints are undocumented with personal-use style ToS (Data sources).
